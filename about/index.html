About

Built inside a working practice.

Statli is a practice dashboard for chiropractic clinics. It is made and run by Statli Pty Ltd, an Australian company.

It started as the reporting behind two chiropractic clinics. Their owner needed one place to see new patients, report-of-findings attendance, care-plan conversion, early dropouts and patient visit average, per practitioner and per clinic, without pulling numbers out of the booking system, the ad accounts and a spreadsheet every week. Those clinics grew to a seven-figure practice in two years, and the dashboard is now offered to other clinics.

Statli connects to Cliniko, Practice Hub, Nookal or Halaxy, alongside Google Ads and Meta Ads. Each clinic's setup is configured to how that practice runs: its appointment types, its care-plan model (or none) and the KPIs it already tracks. Setup takes about ten days and needs very little of the clinic's time.

Pricing is per chiropractor, in Australian dollars, excluding GST. Clinic data stays the clinic's own; see the privacy policy and terms for the detail.
