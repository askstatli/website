These terms cover your use of Statli, the practice dashboard supplied by Statli Pty Ltd. They apply from the day you accept a quote or pay a setup fee. Plain English throughout — where a clause matters to you commercially, it says so.

1What you are buying

Statli is a hosted dashboard that reads data from your practice management system and other services you connect, and presents it as reports. You get an instance of the software, configured for your practice, for as long as your subscription is current.

You are buying access to software and to the configuration work described below. You are not buying the software itself, a copy of its source code, or an exclusive right to any part of it.

The service includes: your configured instance, all screens included in your tier, logins for your staff, hosting, updates I release, and support at the level in your tier.

2Setup

The setup fee covers connecting your systems, mapping your practitioners, locations and appointment types, configuring goals, validating the first period of data against a period you already know, and training your team.

The setup fee is payable before setup begins and is not refundable once configuration work has started, because it is my time rather than a licence charge. If I cannot make your data work — for example your practice management system turns out not to be supported — I will tell you before starting and refund it in full.

Setup needs things only you can provide: API credentials, decisions about how your stages map, and someone available to answer questions. If those are not forthcoming, setup pauses rather than fails, and the subscription does not start until you are live.

3Fees, GST and payment

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and exclude GST, which is added where it applies. The tier you pay for is determined by the number of chiropractors practising in your business.

Subscription is billed monthly in advance from the day you go live, or annually in advance where you have taken the annual option.

  • If your chiropractor count moves into a higher band, the new rate applies from the next billing period after I tell you. I will always tell you before charging more.
  • If it drops into a lower band, tell me and the lower rate applies from the next billing period.
  • Invoices are due within 14 days. If an invoice is more than 30 days overdue I may suspend access after giving you 7 days' written notice. Suspension does not delete your data.

I can change subscription pricing with 60 days' written notice. If you don't accept a change, you may cancel before it takes effect and pay nothing further.

4Term, cancellation and what happens after

After setup, the subscription runs month to month. Either of us may cancel with 30 days' written notice. There is no minimum term and no exit fee.

Annual subscriptions run for the twelve months paid. If you cancel mid-term I will refund unused whole months, less the discount you received for paying annually.

On cancellation you get a full export of everything the dashboard holds for you, in a machine-readable format, before access ends. Your instance and its database are permanently deleted within 30 days of your final day. You should revoke the API credentials you issued — I will remind you, but only you can do it.

I may terminate immediately if you use the service unlawfully, or if fees remain unpaid 60 days after the due date. You still get your export.

Support response times are set by your tier. Support covers the dashboard itself; it does not cover your practice management system, your accounting software, or your advertising accounts.
